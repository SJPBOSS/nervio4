Guides

Nervio is a breathing timer for general wellness. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number or a crisis line. Stop if you feel dizzy or light-headed; if you have a heart, lung or other medical condition or are pregnant, check with a doctor first. Terms and disclaimer

Need help now?

If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, use a line for your country. Nervio is not an emergency service and cannot contact anyone for you.
