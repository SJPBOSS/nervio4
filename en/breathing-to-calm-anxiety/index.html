Breathing to calm anxiety fast

General wellness information. Not medical advice, and not a substitute for professional care.

Fast, here, means the exercise itself is short. It does not mean anxiety disappears on a schedule. The pattern is a longer exhale than inhale, with no breath holds: in for 4 seconds, out for 6. A few minutes is the whole thing.

Calm mode in Nervio starts at that count. You can choose 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes. Eyes open is fine. Matching the guide loosely is fine.

The exercise

Stay where you are if you are safe. You do not need a quiet room, a special posture, or your eyes closed.

  1. 1Inhale for about 4 seconds. A normal breath is enough.
  2. 2Exhale for about 6 seconds. Do not rush it, and do not push until you are straining to be empty.
  3. 3Keep going for the time you chose. If your mind wanders, that is normal. Come back to the longer out-breath when you notice.
  4. 4Stop when the time ends, even if you still feel anxious. You can repeat it later.

If 4 and 6 feel like too much, you are allowed to breathe smaller than the count. The point is the longer out-breath, not a perfect number.

Calm, Reset, and the others

Calm is the everyday timed version. Reset is for a panic attack: shorter breaths, about 3 in and 5 out, no timer, and you stop when you want. Box breathing adds equal holds if you want something to count. 4-7-8 is slower and is meant for bed, not for the middle of a meeting.

After a Calm session you can mark Better, Same, or Worse. Worse shortens the next session a little. Better lengthens it. The exhale stays longer than the inhale.

When anxiety is more than a rough few minutes

This is not therapy, and it is not a plan for panic disorder, trauma, or anxiety that is getting in the way of your life. If that is where you are, a qualified professional can help. If you are in crisis, use the lines at the bottom of this page. In Canada, call or text 988.

Breathing for focus is a different job: a short even pattern before you start a task, not a way to bring anxiety down.

This is not medical care

Nervio shares general wellness information and breathing exercises. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. Stop if you feel pain, faint, or unwell.

If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call 988 or text 988 in Canada (9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline), any time, day or night. In the United States, call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you are somewhere else, contact your local emergency number or crisis line.

If this could be a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the United States).
