Breathing for focus

General wellness information. Not medical advice, and not a substitute for professional care.

Nervio is a breathing timer for general wellness. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number or a crisis line. Stop if you feel dizzy or light-headed; if you have a heart, lung or other medical condition or are pregnant, check with a doctor first. Terms and disclaimer

Focus, here, means a short and even breathing pattern before a task. It is not a trick that does the task for you. Nervio’s Focus mode uses box breathing: four equal parts, 4 seconds each. One or two minutes is enough to mark a start.

This link opens Focus for 2 minutes. The steps of the pattern are on the box breathing page. What follows is how to use that minute.

A small ritual

Put the task where you can see it, then pause before you begin. The pause is the point. You are making an edge between “not started” and “started.”

  1. 1Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, pause 4. That is one box.
  2. 2Keep going for 1 or 2 minutes. Quick, about 30 seconds, is enough if that is all you want.
  3. 3When the session ends, start the task. Do not add extra rounds in order to feel ready. Readiness is not required.
  4. 4If you lose the count, start the next inhale when you notice. Then go back to the work.

If counting makes you tighter

Some people get more tense when they hold their breath or chase a number. If that is you, switch to Calm for a minute: in for 4, out for 6, no holds. Then start the task anyway. Either pattern is a pause, not an exam.

If you are panicking, do not use holds to force focus. Use Reset and come back to the task later.

What not to expect

A breathing app will not hand you attention, and it does not take the place of care for ADHD or any other condition. Sleep, breaks, and the way the work is set up matter more than a perfect count. Use the session as a clean edge, then do one small piece of the task.

The same 4-4-4-4 pattern is explained as box breathing. For bedtime, 4-7-8 is slower on purpose. It is a poor choice right before you need to drive or concentrate.
