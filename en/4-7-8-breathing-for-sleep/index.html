4-7-8 breathing for sleep

General wellness information. Not medical advice, and not a substitute for professional care.

4-7-8 breathing is a slow count people use as a wind-down before sleep: inhale for 4, hold for 7, exhale for 8. The long exhale is the main idea. The hold is only useful if it feels comfortable.

Nervio’s Sleep mode starts at that count. This link opens a 2-minute Sleep session. It is a few quiet minutes, not a promise that you will fall asleep.

The counts

Do this lying down or sitting somewhere you can rest. Do not do it while driving, in water, or anywhere drowsiness would be unsafe. Some people feel lightheaded with a long hold. If that happens, stop and breathe in your usual way.

  1. 1Inhale quietly for 4 seconds. You do not need a dramatic breath.
  2. 2Hold for 7 seconds, and only if that feels all right. If the hold feels tight or scary, skip it.
  3. 3Exhale slowly for 8 seconds, without forcing the last bit of air out.
  4. 4Rest a moment if you need to, then repeat for a few rounds.

If the hold is the part that bothers you, drop it. Calm mode is a simpler bedtime-or-daytime pattern: 4 seconds in, 6 seconds out, no hold at all.

Using Sleep mode

Sleep mode begins at 4 in, 7 hold, 8 out. A full round is 19 seconds, so two minutes is a handful of rounds. Quick is shorter if you only want one or two. When the time ends, you can mark Better, Same, or Worse. Worse makes the next session a little shorter. Better makes it a little longer. The exhale stays the longest part.

Dim the screen if you are already in bed. The phase word is large on purpose, so you can read Inhale, Hold, or Exhale without hunting. Sound is optional.

If sleep is an ongoing problem

A counting pattern will not fix a sleep disorder, pain, a new baby, or a schedule that never lets you rest. A clinician is the right person for that. Use this as a quiet few minutes at the end of the day.

If you are wound up rather than sleepy, box breathing is more even and less drowsy. If you are panicking, use the panic exercise instead of a long hold.

This is not medical care

Nervio shares general wellness information and breathing exercises. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. Stop if you feel pain, faint, or unwell.

If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call 988 or text 988 in Canada (9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline), any time, day or night. In the United States, call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you are somewhere else, contact your local emergency number or crisis line.
