Terms and disclaimer

Not medical advice

Nervio is a breathing timer for general wellness. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and not for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number or a crisis line.

The written guides are general wellness information. They do not tell you what a feeling is, and they are not a substitute for professional care.

Your own risk

Stop if you feel dizzy or light-headed; if you have a heart, lung or other medical condition or are pregnant, check with a doctor first.

You decide whether to use Nervio. You use the timer and the guides at your own risk.

Not for emergencies

Nervio is not for emergencies. If you might need urgent help, call your local emergency number. If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, use a crisis line. The lines below are public lines, not part of the timer.

This is not medical care

Nervio shares general wellness information and breathing exercises. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. Stop if you feel pain, faint, or unwell.

If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call 988 or text 988 in Canada (9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline), any time.

Last checked: October 2026

Other countries
  • United States: call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
  • United Kingdom and Ireland: call the Samaritans free, any time, on 116 123 (Samaritans).
  • Australia: call Lifeline any time on 13 11 14 (Lifeline).
  • New Zealand: call 0800 1737 1737 or text 1737, any time (1737, Need to talk?).
  • Somewhere else: Find a Helpline lists local lines.

If this could be a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the United States, 000 in Australia, 111 in New Zealand).

Free, and as it is

Nervio is free of charge. It is provided as it is. There is no promise that it will always be available, that a count will feel right on every phone, or that it will fit a particular need.
